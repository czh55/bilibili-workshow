有背调支持

When Your Boss Supports the Check

情境讲者给出第一种情形：前主管愿意配合背调，就可以避开被辞退的话题。

01
中文

如果因为个人业绩不佳被辞退，下家面试怎么解释呢？

EN

If you were let go for poor performance, how do you explain it in the next interview?

表达提示业绩不佳：poor performance

02
中文

请上家公司主管支持你，配合你的入职背调。

EN

Ask your former boss to support you in the background check.

表达提示入职背调：background check

03
中文

选择一个恰当的主动离职原因作说辞，比如个人发展或家庭因素。

EN

Use a proper voluntary-reason like personal growth or family matters.

表达提示主动离职原因：voluntary reason

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 背调支持 → 绕开辞退话题

    With the boss's support, you can skip the termination topic.

  2. 换一个体面的主动离职理由

    Swap it for a decent, voluntary reason to leave.

无背调支持

When There's No Support

情境讲者给出第二种情形：前主管不支持背调，只能实话实说但弱化原因、聚焦成长。

01
中文

如果前主管不支持你的背调，我们只能实话实说。

EN

If your former boss won't back you, you have to tell the truth.

表达提示实话实说：tell the truth

02
中文

弱化被辞退的原因，把重点放在你得到的教训上。

EN

Downplay the termination and focus on the lesson you learned.

表达提示弱化原因：downplay the reason

03
中文

讲清楚做了哪些弥补措施，比如培训、充电。

EN

Talk about the steps you took to make up, like training and upskilling.

表达提示弥补措施：steps to make up

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 实话实说 ≠ 全盘交代

    Being honest doesn't mean disclosing everything.

  2. 把焦点从「被辞」转到「成长」

    Shift the focus from being fired to how you grew.

红线原则

The Red Line

情境讲者强调无论多委屈，面试时都绝不说原公司负面信息。

01
中文

一定要记住一个原则：不讲原公司的负面信息。

EN

Always remember one rule: never trash your old company.

表达提示负面信息：trash / bad-mouth

02
中文

不管你认为自己被辞退有多么不公。

EN

No matter how unfair you think the termination was.

表达提示有多么不公：how unfair it was

03
中文

不要讲原公司、原主管、原同事的负面信息。

EN

Don't bad-mouth your former company, boss, or coworkers.

表达提示讲负面：bad-mouth

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 委屈也不讲负面 → 守住格局

    No matter how unfair it feels, keep your composure and stay positive.

  2. 把负面删掉 → 换成成长叙事

    Cut the negativity and replace it with a growth story.

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

表达「被辞退」

I was let go from my last role.

表达「配合背调」

My former manager was willing to support my background check.

表达「我从中学到教训」

Looking back, I learned a valuable lesson from that experience.

表达「做过弥补措施」

I took courses to upskill and improve my performance.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I was fired by the company.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I was let go from my last role.

被辞退用 let go，更中性、不刺耳。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The manager supports my background check.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：My former manager agreed to back me in the background check.

配合背调用 back / support，比 supports my check 完整。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Don't say negative information of old company.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Never bad-mouth your former company.

讲负面信息用 bad-mouth / trash，更口语。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I made up the reason.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I gave a proper voluntary reason.

说辞不是造假，是 give a proper reason。
