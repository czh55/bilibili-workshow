避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Pour hard to force a bigger tulip.
✓ Switch to a bigger pitcher with a wider spout.

Force doesn't fix flow — the spout size controls how much milk releases.

✕ Freehand the position without a reference.
✓ Mark a centerline on the cup, like a rubber band.

A visible line turns vague placement into a repeatable rule.

✕ Hook upward past the centerline for a longer petal.
✓ Hook upward and stop at the line.

Crossing the line breaks the leaf's edge and distorts the shape.

✕ Tilt the pitcher as much as possible.
✓ Tilt enough to control direction and keep flow steady.

Over-tilting floods the surface and washes out the pattern.
