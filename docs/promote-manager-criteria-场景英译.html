Three reasons: bandwidth, backup, and capability

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：His air is to be a boss.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：He has the aura of a boss.

aura 比 air 更能表达「气场」。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：When the team over ten people.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：When the team passes ten people.

用 pass/exceed 表达超过。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Everyone below turns water.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Everyone mutinies / turns against me.

反水要意译为 mutiny 或 turn against。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：He is an opinion person.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：He's an opinion leader.

opinion leader 是固定表达。
