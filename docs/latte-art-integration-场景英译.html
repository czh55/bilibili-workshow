融合做不好，图案再好看也白搭

Bad Integration Ruins Even a Pretty Pattern

情境很多新手把重点全放在拉图案上，但拉花入门的门槛其实是融合。如果融合阶段做不好，后面图案再好看，整杯的口感与视觉都会打折。

融合的本质：让奶泡和咖啡成为一体

The Essence: Foam and Espresso Become One

情境融合的目标是把打发好的奶泡与浓缩咖啡充分混合，让整杯喝起来是『一杯完整的拿铁』，而不是上层泡沫、下层苦水的分层。

What Goes Wrong: Patterns Float on a Split Surface

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Jump straight to the pattern and skip integration.
✓ Fix the integration before you worry about the art.

A great pattern on a split drink still tastes bad.

✕ Judge the drink by the art on top.
✓ Judge it by mouthfeel and flavor throughout.

The design is visual; the quality is tasted.

✕ Pour fast to chase a clean pattern.
✓ Pour slowly and let the foam blend first.

Speed breaks the bond between foam and espresso.

✕ Blame the pattern when the cup separates.
✓ Blame the integration step you rushed.

Separation is a pour problem, not a drawing problem.
