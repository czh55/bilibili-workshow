避坑 PITFALLS

✕ My husband shooted me.
✓ The photos my husband took of me.

「拍的照片」用 photos my husband took，take 的过去分词是 taken。

✕ You face light and he back light.
✓ You face the light, he stands against it.

「背光」用 stands against the light。

✕ Scene messy, person big.
✓ Messy scene makes the person big.

「景乱人大」用 messy scene makes the person big。

✕ Phone slight down.
✓ Lift the phone with a slight inward tilt.

「微微内扣」用 a slight inward tilt。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说拍照只会 take photo→用 mantras（口诀）、face the light（迎光）、vertical lines（竖线）
说构图只会 frame→用 intersection points（交汇点）、upward triangle（正三角）、rule-of-thirds（三分法）
说进步只会 better→用 feel the improvement（有进步）、clicks instantly（一遍就会）、every shot works（随便拍都出片）
