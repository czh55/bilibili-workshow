避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Weakness is raised by doting.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Weakness is indulged into being.

indulged into being 表达「被宠成」。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Strength is rubbed out by life.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Strength is forged by life's grinding.

forge（锻造）比 rub out 更准确地表达「磨出来」。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：They make an appointment to die together.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：They make pacts to die together.

pact to die together 是「相约自杀」的地道说法。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The remaining road is depression.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：What's left is depression or suicide.

what's left is 更口语。
