50万带杠杆的真相

The leveraged truth about 500k

情境讲者回忆新天地西班牙餐厅的肉疼经历，引出老板的杠杆理论。

01
中文

你说年薪50万的工作，是不是干了5份年薪10万的工作？

EN

A 500k-a-year job—is it really just five 100k jobs stacked together?

表达提示年薪：annual salary；干5份：do five of them

当年我年薪刚过10万、非常缺钱的时候，好不容易约了个漂亮的妹子周末逛街，逛到新天地一家西班牙餐厅，打开菜单一看——哎呦，那个价格，现在想想都肉疼。

EN

Back when I'd just crossed the 100k mark and money was tight, I finally scored a date with a cute girl. We strolled into a Spanish restaurant in Xintiandi—and when I opened the menu and saw the paella prices, it stung just thinking about it.

表达提示新天地：Xintiandi (上海地标)；肉疼：it stings, it still hurts

表达提示讨教：pick someone's brain；绝不是：by no means

Lever one: break the role ceiling

从20万到50万，你的工作需要加两个杠杆。第一个杠杆，突破岗位限制。

To go from 200k to 500k, you need to add two levers to your job. The first lever: break the ceiling of your role.

表达提示杠杆：leverage / lever；突破岗位限制：break the role ceiling

如果只做好手边的工作，那你只是一个优秀的螺丝钉；但只要你愿意，你可以把齿轮、钻头、电动机的活，全部装进自己的脑袋里。

If you only do the work right in front of you, you're just a great cog. But if you're willing, you can cram the jobs of the gear, the drill, and the motor all into your own head.

杠杆二：突破技能限制

Lever two: break the skill ceiling

情境讲者讲经理总监级技能让年薪乘2乘3，并用自己的实践验证。

01
中文

第二个杠杆，是突破技能限制。如果只会销售技能，做得再好，也只能是带两三个人的主管；但要做到经理、总监级别，需要财务、战略、公关、演讲、协作、培训等等技能。

EN

The second lever is breaking the skill ceiling. If sales is your only skill, you'll top out as a supervisor with two or three people no matter how good you are. But manager and director level requires finance, strategy, PR, public speaking, collaboration, training, and so on.

表达提示突破技能限制：break the skill ceiling；总监：director；公关：public relations (PR)

02
中文

你的本职岗位工作年薪是10万，但你的其他技能，可以让你的年薪乘以2、乘以3。因为公司要用你这个技能，就不必再花钱另外请个人，对公司来说还是省钱的。

EN

Your core role pays 100k a year, but your other skills can multiply that by two or three. Because if the company can put your skills to work, it doesn't have to hire and pay someone extra—which still saves money.

表达提示本职岗位：core role；乘以2乘以3：multiply by two or three；省钱：save money

03
中文

你看到这条视频就是个例子——写、拍、剪都是我一个人干的。储备技能、一专多能，确实像杠杆一样撬动年薪，同时劳动时间并没有增加。本人亲测管用，推荐给你。

EN

This very video is the proof—writing, filming, editing, all done by me alone. Stocking up on skills and being good at many things really levers up your salary, and your working hours don't grow. I've tried it myself and it works. Give it a shot.

表达提示写拍剪：write, film and edit；一专多能：one specialty, many skills；撬动：lever up；亲测：tried and tested

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 技能单一 → 年薪封顶

    A single skill → a capped salary.

  2. 技能多样 → 年薪乘2乘3

    Multiple skills → a salary multiplied by two or three.

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

表达「年薪50万」

She earns 500k a year—an annual salary of half a million.

说「不是5份10万」

It's not five 100k jobs stacked together.

表达「带杠杆」

Your extra skills act as leverage on your salary.

说「肉疼」

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Is the 500k job doing 5 of the 100k jobs?
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Is a 500k job just five 100k jobs rolled into one?

「干5份」别直译 do five jobs，用 rolled into one / stacked together 更自然。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The price makes my flesh hurt.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The price stings. / It still hurts to think about it.

肉疼 是口语夸张，英文用 sting / hurt，不逐字直译。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：My skills can multiply my salary times 2 times 3.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：My skills can multiply my salary by two or even three.

「乘以2乘以3」→ multiply by two and by three，别写 times 2 times 3。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：You are just an excellent screw.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：You're just a cog in the machine.

螺丝钉 的比喻对应 cog in the machine，而不是物理上的 screw。
