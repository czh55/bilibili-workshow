避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Blame your technique when the art looks messy.
✓ Check the shot concentration first.

A watery shot can't hold art no matter how steady your hand is.

✕ Only add more coffee to boost concentration.
✓ Also try a finer grind or a shorter yield.

Dose, grind, and yield are three equivalent levers on the same dial.

✕ Aim for maximum concentration at all costs.
✓ Balance body for art with a drinkable taste.

Over-extracted shots get bitter even if the art looks good.

✕ Judge concentration by eye color alone.
✓ Watch the flow rate and test each shot side by side.

Flow and body, not just color, tell you whether the shot is right.
