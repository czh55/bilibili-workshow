避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Pour with a big flow during the base.
✓ Pour low and small, at the highest point of the cup bottom.

A heavy early pour punches bubbles into the crema before the surface is ready.

✕ Let the swirl wander to the cup wall.
✓ Keep the ellipse strictly inside the surface.

Hitting the wall dirties the surface and ruins the clean lines.

✕ Keep the spout far from the surface at the end.
✓ Hug the surface and move fast on the final eight.

With wet foam, the milk can't hold on the surface unless the spout is close.

✕ Pause or cut the flow while pushing petals.
✓ Keep a steady flow from petal to petal and finish with a bigger center.

Breaking the flow mid-petal makes the wrap incomplete and uneven.

以前融合流量大、轨迹碰杯壁、收尾离液面远→现在控住流量节奏与液面轨迹：小流量防气泡、轨迹在液面内、贴面快画、连续推瓣
