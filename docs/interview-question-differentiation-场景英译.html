01
中文

EN

The recruiter cares about the role itself — ask about key strategies and performance metrics.

表达提示考核指标：performance metrics

02
中文

EN

The hiring manager usually runs the department — your questions should show big-picture thinking, not nitpicks.

表达提示有高度：big-picture thinking

03
中文

  2. 问题的高度要对上面试官的位置

    Match your questions to the interviewer's level.
