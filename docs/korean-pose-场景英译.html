姿势6：一脚点地一脚半曲

Pose 6: One Foot Pointed, One Half-Bent

情境动作很高的时候，要一条腿自然点地，另外一条腿放到一半，这样显腿长又自然。直接做什么动作都很自然。语域：拍照教学，收尾。

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Make one triangle and two triangles.
✓ One triangle, two triangles — a triangular composition.

「三角构图」用 triangular composition。

✕ Raise your hand to the waist.
✓ Raise your arm to your hip.

「叉腰」用 hand on hip，是 hip 不是 waist。

✕ Put the neck line out.
✓ Reveal the neck and shoulder lines.

「露出肩颈线条」用 reveal the lines。

✕ One leg is half and one is point.
✓ One foot naturally points down and the other bends halfway.

「一点地一半曲」用 point down / bend halfway。
