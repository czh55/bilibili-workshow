避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The event near the Distillery.
✓ The event is near the Distillery District.

句子缺谓语动词 be；near 是介词，不能单独作谓语。

✕ It has a animal rescue station.
✓ It's something like an animal rescue shelter.

shelter（收容所）比 station 更准确；something like 表达「类似」的语气也更自然。

✕ The noodle is super good eat.
✓ The rice noodles are super tasty.

tasty（好吃）是形容词，不能说 good eat；米粉用复数 rice noodles。

✕ I more like to work here.
✓ I'd be more willing to come work here.

be willing to（愿意做某事）表达倾向；I more like 是中式直译，不地道。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说好吃只会 delicious→用 super tasty、amazing、pretty solid、越吃越好吃 the more the tastier
说好看只会 beautiful→用 nice、pretty、golden（金色的）、even nicer 表达程度对比
说陪狗玩只会 play→用 walk the dog（遛狗）、wander around（散步）、hang out（待在一起）
