避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The success rate raises by 90%.
✓ Your success rate shoots up by 90%.

shoot up 表示「飙升」，raise 表示及物「举起」

✕ Feet must below the line.
✓ Keep your feet below the line.

below 是介词，前面要有 be 动词或动词

✕ Squat down and shoot from low.
✓ Squat down a little and shoot from below.

from below 是「从低处」，from low 不地道

✕ Combine them to a long picture.
✓ Combine them into one long image.

combine A into B 才是「拼成」
