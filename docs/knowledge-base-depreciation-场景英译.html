避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Collect notes just to hoard them.
✓ Judge the base by useful fuel, not note count.

A thousand notes with fewer than fifty useful means the material, not the tool, is the problem.

✕ Force yourself to write regularly no matter what.
✓ Lower the friction first — talk to your notes instead.

Blank-page cognitive load makes people quit or fall back to dead journaling.

✕ Rely on AI search to fix weak notes.
✓ Fix the fuel quality, then let search work.

Search can find what you saved, but it can't ignite dead data.

✕ Spend hours on folders and tags.
✓ Let the base self-organize through conversation.

The experiment shows structure can emerge without manual organizing.
