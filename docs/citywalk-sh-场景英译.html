Hi everyone, I'm photographer Senhua, and today we're doing a Citywalk in Shanghai.

引导模特摆坐姿

Sit on the wall, don't lean, prop your arms back and rest your head on it.

向路人借道具

Excuse me, may I borrow your watering can for a photo?

拍摄结束道别

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The camera is off itself.
✓ The camera shut itself off.

「自动关机」是 shut itself off / auto shut down，不是 off itself（把 off 当动词是错的）。

✕ Walk to here.
✓ Walk toward me / come over here.

walk to here 不自然，用 come over here 或 walk toward me。

✕ I want to borrow a watering can take photo.
✓ May I borrow your watering can to take a photo?

borrow ... to do / for ... 结构；两个动词不能直接堆叠。

✕ This pose is very good-looking.
✓ That pose looks great / very nice.

形容「人好看/姿势好看」用 looks great / flattering；good-looking 通常修饰人而非姿势。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

引导模特只会说 stand here / sit here→用描述性口令：don't lean、prop your arms back、rest your head、get down lower
说天气只会 rainy→区分 overcast（阴天）、drizzly（毛毛雨）、sunny spells（间歇放晴）
说设备只会 camera→按器材区分 action camera（运动相机）、prime lens（定焦）、wide aperture（大光圈）
