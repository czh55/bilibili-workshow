避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Make the sunset more red.
✓ Make the sunset redder.

红色更红用比较级 redder，不用 more red

✕ Opposing colors mix become white.
✓ Opposing colors mixed together turn white.

mixed together 是过去分词短语，作后置定语

✕ Not enough of your color, it's the opposite.
✓ It's not that your color is lacking — it's the opposite.

it's not that...it's that... 是固定句式

✕ Cyan is mixed by blue and green.
✓ Cyan is made by mixing blue and green.

be made by + doing 才表示「由…混合而成」

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

颜色不够就加颜色→先抽走混进来的对立色
拉满单色想加深→拉满反而变白，补色抵消
调橙色凭感觉→近少远多，按距离增减
