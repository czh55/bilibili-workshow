避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Transparent, everyone has no interest to take.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Once it's all transparent, there's nothing in it for them.

「没有利益可拿」不能直译，nothing in it for someone 是地道口语。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：How does the system land?
✓ ✓ 地道说法：How do we actually roll out the system?

「落地」是实施的意思，用 roll out / implement，不能直译 land。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：You are high above, you will die.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Act high and mighty, and you're dead in the water.

「高高在上」对应 high and mighty；「一定死」口语说 dead in the water。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：We can't say the true words.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：We can't tell them the whole story.

「真话」对应 the whole story / the full picture，比 true words 地道。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为「真诚直说」能换来信任→要先把目的包装成「帮忙」，才能换来配合
