情况一与二

Scenarios One and Two

情境讲者说明公司原因可直说，个人原因且有背调支持可换主动离职原因。

01
中文

公司结构性裁员或经济性裁员，与工作表现无关，可以实话实说。

EN

If it was a structural or economic layoff, it has nothing to do with your performance—be honest.

02
中文

表达提示配合背调：back the check

03
中文

    Company-caused layoffs are safe to admit.

  2. 背调支持 → 换成主动离职

    With check support, reframe it as a voluntary move.

情况三：有选择地说

Scenario Three: Choose Your Words

情境讲者说明主管不支持背调时，只能有选择地实话实说，弱化原因聚焦成长。

01
中文

EN

表达提示背景调查：background check

02
中文

EN

表达提示弱化：downplay

03
中文

讲清楚弥补措施，比如去培训、去充电。

EN

表达提示弥补措施：steps to make up

  1. 诚实是底线 → 叙事是策略

    Honesty is the floor; framing is the strategy.

  2. 焦点 → 从被辞转到成长

    Shift the spotlight from being fired to your growth.

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

表达「公司裁员」

It was a company-wide layoff, not about my performance.

表达「主动离职的原因」

I decided to leave for personal growth reasons.

表达「我学到了教训」

That experience taught me a valuable lesson.

表达「弥补与进步」

I've taken courses and improved a lot since then.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The company cut me off.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I was laid off in a company restructuring.

被裁用 laid off in a restructuring，更具体。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I was dismissed because of personal reason.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I left, and I can explain the reasons.

个人原因场景下避免被动词 dismissed。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The manager does the endorsement.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：My manager was willing to vouch for me.

背书用 vouch for，而不是 endorsement。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：We should selectively say.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Be honest, but choose your words carefully.

有选择地说用 choose your words carefully。
