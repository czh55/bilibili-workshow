避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The city was built in chronological order.
✓ I like to explore the city in chronological order of its history.

chronological 形容的是『按时间排列的顺序』，主语是人（去逛的人），不是城市本身。

✕ The church is on the mosque.
✓ The cathedral was built on the foundations of a former mosque.

on the mosque 字面是『在清真寺上面』，不表达『在原址基础上重建』；要用 on the foundations of / on the site of。

✕ The plaza has 48 colorful holes.
✓ The plaza has 48 colorful alcoves.

alcove 是『壁龛』，hole 是『洞』，两者的视觉含义完全不同。

✕ Rome came to Spain first here.
✓ This was Rome's first settlement in Spain.

settlement（定居点）比 came to（来到）更精确；表达历史事实用名词化结构更地道。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说建筑古老只会 old→区分 ancient（古罗马）、historical（历史性）、futuristic（未来主义）、centuries-old（几百年历史）
说时间线只会 timeline→用 chronological order（时间顺序）、the Age of Exploration（大航海时代）、an era（一个时代）更有历史厚度
说好/不好只用 good/bad→用 spectacular、sarcastic、famous、enormous 等更有画面感的词描述建筑与感受
