避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Move closer to the window.
✓ Turn sideways to the window.

Side light creates shadows and depth; facing the light keeps the face flat.

✕ Raise the ISO when it's dark.
✓ Add a controllable light instead.

Higher ISO also brightens the background and kills the separation.

✕ Always shoot at 50mm.
✓ Pick the focal length by room size; 35mm for small spaces.

50mm needs about 1.5m camera distance plus 2-3m of backdrop space.

✕ Use as many lights as possible.
✓ Control light position and color temperature first.

Texture comes from light ratios and color gaps, not lamp count.
