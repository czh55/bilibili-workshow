谜题：4K 为什么不如 1080P 清晰

The Puzzle: Why 4K Looks Less Sharp Than 1080P

情境4K 与 1080P 对比，4K 反而不清晰。原因在光：光线不足产生噪点、细节模糊、主体背景糊在一起。

EN

This is 4K footage, and this is 1080P. Why does the 4K look less sharp than the 1080P?

表达提示4K footage（4K画面）

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Buy a 4K camera and expect sharp video automatically.
✓ Fix the light first — 4K without light is still soft.

Resolution sets the ceiling, light decides what you actually see.

✕ Evenly brighten the whole scene to make it sharp.
✓ Create contrast: light the subject more than the background.

Even brightness has no depth; subject and background stay merged.

✕ Rely on post-processing to fix everything.
✓ Get the contrast in-camera, use post only as a rescue.

No contrast captured means nothing to rescue later.
