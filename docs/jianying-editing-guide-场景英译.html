Rough Cut: Empty-Shot Openings and In-Out Points

情境片头先放空镜头营造氛围，不直接放人物镜头。按I键入点、O键出点拖到时间线；同场景成组、异场景加空镜过渡；分离音频删除原声去杂音。

精剪：对齐节奏与变速

Fine Cut: Aligning Beats and Changing Speed

情境素材拖到自由轨道逐段调整，Command+B分割、Delete删除，与音乐节奏点对齐。Command+R变速：人物0.25倍、空镜0.5倍，需120帧素材。

表达提示a free track（自由轨道）

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Start with a people shot and dive straight in.
✓ Open with empty shots to set the mood.

Too-fast openings make viewers swipe away.

✕ Slow the footage without checking the frame rate.
✓ Confirm it's 120fps before going to 0.25x.

30/60fps footage stutters when slowed.

✕ Add transitions between every single clip.
✓ Only between different scenes.

Same-scene shots read as one group — transitions feel forced.

✕ Crank the SFX volume.
✓ Keep effects low as garnish.

Sound should whisper atmosphere, not shout over the picture.
