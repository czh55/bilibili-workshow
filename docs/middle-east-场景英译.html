避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The mosque has no top.
✓ The mosque was designed without a dome.

「没有圆顶」是 without a dome / dome-less，不是 no top。

✕ The tomb is very old.
✓ These tombs were carved bit by bit by the Nabataeans.

说明建造方式（carve bit by bit）比只说 old 更有历史信息。

✕ The building reflects.
✓ The mirrored facade reflects the surrounding rocks and desert.

主语应是建筑立面（facade），reflect 后面要有宾语（倒映什么）。

✕ Saudi is very modern.
✓ Saudi Arabia blends cutting-edge modernity with its ancient faith.

blends A with B 表达「共存」比 very modern 更精准。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS
