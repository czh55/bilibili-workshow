避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The fashion week is once a year.
✓ I take an annual trip to Europe for fashion week.

once a year 形容频率，但『一年一度的旅行』更自然用 annual trip；主语是人。

✕ This room is very pretty.
✓ Wow, this room is gorgeous.

gorgeous 比 pretty 更强烈，更贴合 Vlog 里看到漂亮房间的惊叹感。

✕ I don't know how to say pork neck.
✓ I don't even know how to describe this pork neck.

describe（描述味道/口感）比 say（怎么说这个词）更贴切语境。

✕ We're in same class since kindergarten.
✓ We were in the same class from kindergarten through high school.

时间跨度用 from A through B；since 只接时间点，不能接一个区间。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说漂亮只会 pretty / beautiful→按程度分：lovely、gorgeous、stunning、adorable（可爱）
说好吃只会 delicious→用 unbelievably good、super tasty、authentic（正宗）、to die for
说一起玩只会 play together→用 hang out（出去玩）、be in the same class、spend time together 表达不同场景
