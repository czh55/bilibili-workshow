高考只是人生的开始

The gaokao is just the start

情境讲者宽慰考生：高考只是开始，招生比例提高、不必与他人比较。

01
中文

高考就是上一个大学，这是人生的开始；你的人生怎么发展，不是由这次高考决定的。

EN

The gaokao just gets you into college. It's the beginning of your life, and where your life goes from here isn't decided by this one exam.

表达提示人生的开始：the beginning of life；决定：decide

02
中文

现在招生比例比以前大了很多，每个人考上的概率几乎是很大的，所以不要担心自己考不上。

EN

Admission ratios are much higher now than in our day, so your odds of getting in are quite high — don't worry that you won't make it.

表达提示招生比例：admission ratio；考上：get in

03
中文

不必非要和同学比、考最高的分、上最好的大学；哪怕上一般大学，最后也能做出很伟大的事情。

EN

You don't have to outdo your classmates, get the top score, or attend the best university; even an ordinary university can still lead to great things.

表达提示和别人比：compare with others；最高的分：the top score

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 高考决定人生 → 高考只是开始

    The gaokao decides your life vs. it's only the beginning.

  2. 怕考不上就完了 → 录取率高，不必担心

    Fearing you'll fail vs. knowing the odds are on your side.

大学好坏只是维度之一

Ranking is just one dimension

情境讲者讲普通大学的优势：竞争压力小、自信和兴趣更大。

01
中文

上最好的大学和上一般的大学确实有差别，但对人生的影响不是定乾坤的，人生还有很多次机会，才刚刚开始。

EN

Going to the best university or an ordinary one does make a difference, but it doesn't decide the course of your whole life — life is full of chances, and yours has just begun.

表达提示定乾坤：decide the whole course；机会：chance, opportunity

02
中文

大学的好与坏，也是一种排行，只是其中的一个维度而已，其实有多个维度。

EN

A university's reputation is just a ranking, and ranking is only one dimension of it — there are actually many dimensions.

表达提示排行：ranking；维度：dimension

03
中文

反倒一些普通大学里竞争压力小，你的兴趣和自信心会更大；放下必须拿最高分的心态，你就不紧张了。

EN

At an ordinary university the competition is less fierce, so your interests and confidence can grow more. Once you let go of the mindset that you must have the top score, you stop feeling anxious.

表达提示竞争压力：competitive pressure；自信心：self-confidence；放下心态：let go of the mindset

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 排名好 = 大学好

    A good ranking equals a good university.

  2. 非名校不可 → 普通大学也有优势

    Only top schools count vs. ordinary ones have their own strengths.
S301:25–01:55

不紧张不等于放任

Calm doesn't mean slack

情境讲者澄清不紧张不等于放任，人生严肃要认真对待每件事。

01
中文

不紧张不等于放任，不是让你一天到晚玩、不怎么复习就去高考。

EN

Staying calm doesn't mean letting yourself go — it doesn't mean playing around all day and walking into the gaokao barely prepared.

表达提示放任：let oneself go；不怎么复习：barely prepared

02
中文

面对高考就像面对人生中的任何事情，人生其实是很严肃的，如何有意义地度过人生很重要。

EN

Face the gaokao the way you'd face anything else in life. Life is actually serious, and how you live it meaningfully matters a great deal.

表达提示严肃的：serious；有意义地度过：live meaningfully

03
中文

你才十七八岁，人生已经开始了，要对每件事认真去做；高考也要勤奋地尽最大力量去复习、去准备。

EN

You're only seventeen or eighteen, but your life has already begun, so take everything seriously. For the gaokao, study diligently and give your very best to review and prepare.

表达提示勤奋的：diligent；尽最大力量：give your very best

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 不紧张 = 放任

    Being calm means letting yourself go.

  2. 人生随便过 → 人生要严肃而有意义地过

    Living carelessly vs. living seriously and meaningfully.

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

Exam anxiety often comes from comparing yourself with others.

Admission ratios are much higher than they used to be.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 中式英文：The gaokao decided your future.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The gaokao doesn't decide your future.

decide 的主语是考试，否定别漏 not；中文「不是由……决定」直译时容易丢掉否定。

✕ ✕ 中式英文：Don't worry you can't pass the exam.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Don't worry that you won't get in.

「考上大学」英文用 get in / get admitted，pass the exam 只表示「通过考试」。

✕ ✕ 中式英文：I want to compare with classmates.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I keep comparing myself with my classmates.

拿自己和别人比要用 compare oneself with，compare 不能直接接人当宾语。

✕ ✕ 中式英文：You should let go your mindset.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：You should let go of that mindset.

let go of 是固定搭配，介词 of 不可省略。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

把高考看作「一锤定音的终局」→把高考看作「人生众多机会之一」
把「不紧张」理解成「无所谓、放任」→把「不紧张」理解为「放下执念但认真对待」
用「排名」单维度评价大学→从竞争、兴趣、自信等多维度看大学
