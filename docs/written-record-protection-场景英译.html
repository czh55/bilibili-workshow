约定周报

Setting up a weekly update

情境老板说只要结果，讲者建议主动约定每周发进度表，风险类问题才当面汇报。

01
中文

有的上级说，我更关心这个事情的结果，不要什么事都来找我。

EN

Some managers say: I only care about results — don't come to me with everything.

表达提示目标导向：results-driven

02
中文

遇到你自己解决不了的问题，比如风险、资源，你肯定要跟他讲。

EN

When you hit something you can't solve — a risk or missing resources — you have to tell him.

表达提示风险/资源：risks and resources

03
中文

我每周把项目的进度写成项目管理表发给你。

EN

I'll send you a weekly project status table.

表达提示项目管理表：project status table

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 只要结果 ≠ 不用汇报

    Results-only doesn't mean no updates.

  2. 汇报边界自己约清楚

    Set the reporting boundaries yourself.
S200:51-02:10

带证据汇报

Reporting with written evidence

情境遇到搞不定的风险要简明汇报并拿老板表态，被反问就出示沟通记录与会议纪要。

01
中文

如果这个问题不解决，有可能会延迟个四五天，你那边觉得OK吗？

EN

If we don't fix this, it may slip four or five days — are you okay with that?

表达提示延迟四五天：slip four or five days

02
中文

我已经跟他们沟通了两次，会议纪要我已经发给你了。

EN

I've talked to them twice, and I've already sent you the meeting notes.

表达提示会议纪要：meeting notes

03
中文

他们那边老板说必须您出面才行，我的影响力太小，搞不定。

EN

Their manager said only you can step in — my influence is too small to handle it.

表达提示影响力太小：not enough influence

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 汇报让决策留痕

    Updates leave a paper trail for decisions.

  2. 把「他说过」变成「记录里写着」

    Turn 'he said so' into 'it's on record.'
S302:11-03:31

预警升级，保护自己

Escalating alerts to protect yourself

情境讲者用颜色预警机制和周报的合法性，讲如何让责任透明、保护自己。

01
中文

这个项目本来是绿色，然后变黄了，黄的意思就是这是个风险。

EN

The project was green, then turned yellow — meaning it's now flagged as a risk.

表达提示黄色预警：flag as a risk

02
中文

一周不管变红，再一周不管就超期爆雷。

EN

Leave it a week and it turns red; another week and it blows past the deadline.

表达提示超期爆雷：blow the deadline

03
中文

周报就是保护你们的，每周都要发预警给你老板。

EN

Weekly reports protect you — send the alerts to your boss every week.

表达提示保护你：protect you

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 让责任透明化

    Make responsibility visible.

  2. 预警=自保工具

    Alerts are your self-protection tool.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The project turns yellow.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The project is now flagged as a risk.

变黄→被标记为风险，英文用 flagged。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The meeting record has been sent to you.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The meeting notes are already in your inbox.

会议纪要→meeting notes / minutes。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I want to protect my back.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Cover yourself with a paper trail.

保护自己→cover yourself / paper trail。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Don't do anything else when one thing is stuck.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Don't put everything on hold over one blocker.

一件事卡住≠所有事停摆。
