避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Keep your short-video ease on live.
✓ Train command of the room before going live.

Real-time streaming has no cuts — a flat or stiff state exposes you instantly.

✕ Recite product specs as your script.
✓ Build a script with scenes, pain points, and benefits.

Spec lists read like a manual and kill the human touch viewers pay for.

✕ Yell "last two, last three!" to close.
✓ Give a reason first, then add urgency naturally.

Hollow urgency sounds forced and viewers tune out on the spot.
