避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：You don't need to manage money before 5 million.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：You don't need to invest before five million—fixed deposits are just fine.

manage money 是中式搭配，英文里谈投资用 invest 更自然。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Two points is 100,000 yuan.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Two percentage points on five million is a hundred thousand yuan.

two points 单独说容易歧义，需要带本金基数。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Raise salary and position.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Get promoted and get raises.

promote 与 raise 是固定搭配，且 raise 作「加薪」通常是复数。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：You have this heart to do your own work.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Put that energy into your day job.

「有这个心思」直译生硬，用 put energy into 更地道。
