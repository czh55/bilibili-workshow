竞业协议是什么

What a non-compete actually is

情境讲者解答后台高频问题，先讲清竞业协议用于哪三类人、现在为什么全员都要签。

01
中文

竞业协议作为公司保护它自身商业利益非常重要的手段，通常能用在三类人身上。

EN

A non-compete is a key tool companies use to protect their business interests, and it typically applies to three groups of people.

表达提示保护商业利益：protect business interests

02
中文

现在越来越多公司为了最大化保护自身利益，都放开了标准，要求全员签竞业协议。

EN

Now more and more companies are loosening the bar to protect themselves and require everyone to sign one.

表达提示放开标准：loosen the bar / relax the requirements

03
中文

竞业协议就像一把枷锁，牢牢地锁住员工，很多人不敢轻易地去离职。

EN

A non-compete is like a shackle that locks employees in place, and many are afraid to quit.

表达提示像一把枷锁：like a shackle

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 竞业=锁住跳槽的枷锁

    A non-compete is a shackle on switching jobs.

  2. 适用范围从高管扩大到全员

    It now applies to everyone, not just executives.
S200:21-01:05

违约的代价

The real cost of breaking it

情境讲者给出判断：能不签最好、补签要权衡；岗位关键就老老实实履行，用百度员工的真实案例说明违约代价。

01
中文

离职的时候要求补签竞业协议，你就要去权衡公司给到的补偿和竞业对未来职业发展的影响。

EN

If they ask you to sign one on your way out, weigh the compensation against how the non-compete will limit your career.

表达提示权衡补偿与制约：weigh the pay against the limits

02
中文

如果你所在的岗位非常关键或者比较敏感，老老实实履行竞业协议是最好的方式。

EN

If your role is critical or sensitive, honestly honoring the non-compete is the best move.

表达提示关键岗位：a critical role

03
中文

一旦被发现违约，你需要付出比竞业补偿金高 N 倍的代价，得不偿失。

EN

If you're caught breaching it, you'll pay N times the compensation you'd have gotten — it's not worth it.

表达提示得不偿失：not worth the trouble

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 补签先权衡→制约大于补偿就协商解除

    When signing late, compare the limits and the pay before deciding.

  2. 违约代价≈补偿金×N，别心存侥幸

    The penalty is a multiple of the compensation — don't gamble on it.

两条出路

Two ways out

情境讲者说明换马甲躲不过背调，而公司三个月不付补偿金时劳动者可以书面解除并仲裁。

01
中文

有人说离职换个马甲，比如换个身份或者挂靠给第三方公司，不就可以避免了吗？

EN

People ask: why not switch to a shell — a new identity or a third-party company — to dodge it?

表达提示换马甲：switch to a shell / use a cover

02
中文

事实上圈子就这么大，如果公司真的有心想查你，你的简历还是会被拦截住的。

EN

But it's a small world — if the company really wants to dig, your background will still get flagged.

表达提示圈子就这么大：it's a small world

03
中文

离职三个月内企业没有付补偿金，就发书面解除通知，再申请劳动仲裁确认解除。

EN

If they haven't paid compensation within three months, send a written notice to terminate and then file for arbitration.

表达提示书面解除通知：a written termination notice

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 换马甲≠自由→背调会拦截

    A shell identity doesn't set you free — background checks catch it.

  2. 欠费三个月→书面解除＋仲裁

    Three months unpaid → written notice plus labor arbitration.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Non-compete is like a handcuff locking employees.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：A non-compete is like a shackle that locks employees in place.

shackle 更口语也更形象，handcuff 更偏向具体手铐。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Change a vest to avoid it.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Switch to a shell company to dodge it.

换马甲用 switch to a shell / use a cover 表达。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The circle is just this big.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：It's a small world.

口语里表达「圈子小」直接说 it's a small world。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Pay N times the money of compensation.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Pay N times what you'd have gotten in compensation.

倍数比较用 what you'd have gotten 更自然。
