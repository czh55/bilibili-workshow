今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

介绍植物园的奇妙设计

The founder put cacti and rainforest plants together in one garden by the sea.

解释岩石形成温室

The rocks absorb the sun's heat by day and release it slowly, like a greenhouse.

推荐看果冻海

If you want crystal-clear jelly-sea, come to the Mediterranean.

讲防御海堡的历史

The townspeople built these walls because North African pirates raided the coast.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The plants live in the same place.
✓ Over four thousand plant groups live together in one garden.

用具体数字 plant groups（植物类群）比泛泛 same place 更有信息量。

✕ The rock makes heat.
✓ The rocks absorb heat by day and release it slowly.

makes heat 不通；吸收后缓慢释放是 absorb ... and release ...。

✕ I want to see the ocean blue.
✓ If you want to see the jelly-sea, come to the Mediterranean.

jelly-sea（果冻海）是形容清澈海水的形象说法，比 the ocean blue 更具体。

✕ Pirates were very bad.
✓ North African pirates ran rampant along the coast.

run rampant（猖獗）比 very bad 精准，表现海盗的肆无忌惮。
