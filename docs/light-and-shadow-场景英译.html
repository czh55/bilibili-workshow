避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Because of the light makes it messy.
✓ The messy look comes from the too-even light.

because of 后接名词短语，不接句子

✕ A big lamp light everything evenly.
✓ A big lamp lights everything evenly.

主语第三人称单数，谓语加 s

✕ The lit areas will outstanding.
✓ The lit areas will stand out.

stand out 是动词短语，outstanding 是形容词

✕ Paint the details to black.
✓ Paint the details pure black.

paint + 宾语 + 形容词补语，不用 to

关灯怕拍不清楚→让影去吞没细节，主次立现
废片只能删→重塑光影，废片也能救
