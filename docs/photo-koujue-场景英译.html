避坑 PITFALLS

✕ For a front shot, raise your chin.
✓ For a front shot, lower your chin to define your jawline.

正面机位是低头（lower your chin），抬头反而显脸大。

✕ For a back shot, just stand still.
✓ Flick your head around and glance back — it catches a dynamic moment.

背面照要点在「动」，甩头回眸才有生命力。

✕ I tilt my head for every shot.
✓ The head move depends on the camera angle: chin down, chin up, flick, tilt, or lean forward.

头位跟随机位：每个角度配的动作不同，不能一招打天下。

✕ A low-angle shot always looks bad on me.
✓ Lean your head forward to ease the face compression — it works for low-angle shots.

仰拍显脸大头小，探头可缓解面部压缩。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

只会说 take a photo→用 front shot / profile shot / back shot 按机位区分拍照类型
只会说 turn your head→用 lower chin（低头）、lift chin（抬头）、flick head（甩头）、tilt head（歪头）、lean forward（探头）细分
只会说 look good→用 define the jawline（压出下颌线）、lengthen the neck line（拉长颈线）说清楚「为什么好看」
