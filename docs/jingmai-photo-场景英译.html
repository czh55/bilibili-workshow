避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The X2D2 has continuous focus auto.
✓ The X2D2 has continuous autofocus.

autofocus 是一个词（自动对焦），不能说 focus auto；形容词 continuous 修饰名词 autofocus。

✕ The owner says it don't sell.
✓ The owner said they're not for sale.

be not for sale 是固定表达「不卖」；it don't sell 语法与语义都不对。

✕ I pick a plastic bag.
✓ I picked up a plastic bag from the ground.

pick up（捡起）与 pick（挑选）含义不同；从地上捡要用 pick up。

✕ The weather is not very ideal.
✓ The weather isn't ideal today.

not very ideal 中的 very 冗余；直接说 not ideal 更地道。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说拍得好看只会 good→用 nice、pretty、fun、interesting 表达不同层次
说天气不好只会 bad weather→用 overcast（阴天）、not ideal（不理想）、rainy（下雨）更精准
说捡东西只会 pick→用 pick up（捡起）、gather（采集）、find（发现）区分动作
