问婚育的两个出发点

Why interviewers ask about marriage and kids

情境讲者拆解面试官问婚姻状况的两个真正用意：工作精力投入度和稳定性。

01
中文

我们女生在求职的时候被问到婚姻状况，怎么回答比较合适呢？

EN

When we're asked about our marital status during a job hunt, how should we answer?

表达提示婚姻状况：marital status

02
中文

问这个问题其实主要是两方面的考量：一个就是你在工作上的精力投入度。

EN

There are really two things behind this question: first, how much energy you can put into work.

表达提示精力投入度：energy commitment to work

03
中文

二个就是你的稳定性。

EN

Second, how stable you'll be.

表达提示稳定性：how stable you'll be

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 问婚育≠打探私生活→问两个工作变量

    It's not prying into your life; it's two work-related variables.

  2. 答婚育=回答精力和稳定性

    Answering it means addressing energy and stability.

未婚未孕与已婚已孕的答法

Scripts for single and married-with-kids candidates

情境讲者给出未婚未孕（有对象）和已婚已孕两种情况的回答重点。

01
中文

未婚未孕有对象的话，回答重点要传递两方面的信息：最重要的就是告诉面试官你近期的工作计划。

EN

If you're single with a partner, focus on two things: most importantly, your near-term work plan.

表达提示近期工作计划：near-term work plan

02
中文

其次要表明你的强稳定性，比如说已经在这里定居了等等。

EN

Second, show strong stability — for example, that you're already settled here.

03

    Married with kids: talk goals and leading by example.

已婚未孕与参考项真相

The married-no-kids script and the truth

情境讲者给出已婚未孕的答法，并点破婚育只是参考项、能力素养才决定录用。

01
中文

已婚未孕，回答时一定要表达想先打好物质基础和职业基础，近期没有生育计划。

EN

If you're married without kids, stress that you want to build your financial and career foundations first and have no baby plans soon.

表达提示打好基础：build the foundations

02
中文

虽然上面这些话都是面上的套话，但是你还是得说，而且要说得彼此都很舒服。

EN

These are all face-saving scripts, but you still have to say them — and say them so both sides feel comfortable.

表达提示面上的套话：face-saving scripts

03
中文

婚姻状况只是一个参考项，决定录不录你的关键因素取决于你的专业能力和个人综合素养。

EN

Marital status is just a reference; what decides the offer is your professional skill and overall quality.

表达提示综合素养：overall quality / all-round capability

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 已婚未孕→先基础＋家人共识

    Married without kids: foundations first, family on board.

  2. 套话说舒服→但能力才是底牌

    Say the script smoothly, but competence is the real card.

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

表达「近期规划」

In the next one to three years, my focus is on hitting my career goals.

表达「已定居」

I'm settled in this city, so you don't need to worry about stability.

表达「家人一致」

My family and I are on the same page about my career plans.

表达「参考项」

I believe what matters most is my ability and how well I fit the role.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I will not marry or have kids in two years.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I have no plans for marriage or kids in the near term.

用 have no plans 表达「没有计划」，比否定句承诺更自然可信。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：My child is seen by my parents.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：My parents help take care of the child.

被老人看用 help take care of，避免被动生硬。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Marriage status is only a reference item.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Marital status is just a reference point.

reference point 更口语。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I want to lay a good material foundation first.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I want to build my financial and career foundations first.

build foundations 是固定搭配。
