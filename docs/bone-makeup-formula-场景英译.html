避坑 PITFALLS

✕ I sweep my eyeshadow on directly.
✓ Don't sweep it straight on — press the shadow in first, then blend upward.

眼影要先按上去再向上晕染，直接扫会把粉扫到未定妆的脸颊。

✕ I apply the same highlight for everyone.
✓ Pick the highlight tone by your skin: warm for yellow undertones, cool for fair skin.

提亮颜色要跟肤色：黄皮用暖调、白皮用冷调。

✕ I line outside my lips and stop there.
✓ After lining outside the lip line, blend inward with a small brush.

唇线画完后必须向内晕染，否则会化成大嘴巴。

✕ I'm afraid to contour because powder setting will erase it.
✓ Cream contour fades a little under setting powder but stays — don't be afraid to apply it.

膏状修容定妆后会变淡但不会被盖掉，可以放心画。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

只会说 contour my face→用 inner and outer contours（内外轮廓）、parallelogram of shadow（平行四边形阴影）、fold depth（折叠度）精确描述
只会说 make my nose higher→用 deepen the natural shadow（沿本身阴影加深）、V shape on the nose tip（鼻头V字形）、horizontal line（横画一笔）
只会说 my makeup looks flat→用 contrast of light and shadow（明暗对比）、highlight the brow bone（眉骨提亮）、diagonal blush（斜打腮红）说明立体感来源
