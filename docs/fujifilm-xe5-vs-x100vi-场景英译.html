避坑 PITFALLS

✕ It covers 23mm focus length.
✓ It covers a 23mm focal length.

焦段是 focal length 而不是 focus length；focus 是「对焦」动词/名词。

✕ The camera is very small and light weight.
✓ The camera is compact and lightweight.

compact 与 lightweight 是形容词，直接用即可；light weight 分开写是名词短语。

✕ You can change the lens to a better blur.
✓ You can swap to a lens with better bokeh.

bokeh（虚化效果）是摄影术语；blur 更口语化、不够专业。

✕ If you want beautiful camera, buy it.
✓ If you want a beautiful camera, the X100VI is for you.

可数名词 camera 前需要冠词 a；完整句更需要明确的谓语/结构。
