两种 LUT 用法：放开头还是结尾

Two Ways to Use a LUT: Front or End

情境有人把 LUT 放在调色链路最开头，之后再校色。这是最常见的做法之一，但调色师随即给出一个警告：这可能很危险。

开头的风险：显示器看不到的色会在 P3 里显形

The Risk: Invisible Colors Can Surface in P3

情境如果影片最终要在 DCI P3 色域的投影机播放，显示器上看不到的颜色可能在 P3 里突然显现。对策是在链路末尾加 DCI P3 limiter，把色彩约束在交付色域内。

作者习惯：LUT 放链路末尾，先校色后套 LUT

My Habit: LUT at the End, Correct First

情境作者个人倾向把 LUT 放在链路末尾：先校色、后套 LUT，全程知道自己调色板的约束、对比曲线与最终色彩，非常省时且清晰。两种方式都成立。

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Place the LUT up front with no safety net.
✓ Add a DCI P3 limiter at the end of the chain.

Without a limiter, hidden colors can blow out in the P3 projector.

✕ Trust your monitor for final color.
✓ Verify in your target color space.

Your monitor can't show every color your deliverable can.

✕ Treat LUT placement as pure taste.
✓ Treat it as a workflow decision tied to your color space.

Placement is about managing color-space risk, not preference.

✕ Forget to check the final deliverable.
✓ Confirm your output stays inside the intended color space.

The whole point is a clean, predictable final deliverable.
