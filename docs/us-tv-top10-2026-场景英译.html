The casting was spot-on — even the source author praised the adaptation.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The drama is your dish.
✓ This drama is right up your alley.

your dish 不是「你的菜」的英译；英文用 your cup of tea 或 right up your alley。

✕ The brawl is very 肉搏.
✓ The corridor brawl was raw and physical.

肉搏不要音译，用 brawl / hand-to-hand combat / physical fight。

✕ The show makes people close mouth.
✓ The show makes both fans and casual viewers shut up.

make someone close their mouth 中式直译；shut up（闭嘴/无话可说）更地道，但注意语气。

✕ The special effect is plastic.
✓ The CGI feels cheap and fake.

塑料特效指廉价感，用 cheap/fake/lifeless CGI，而不是字面 plastic。
