Three bonus points and your interest

EN

中文

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 加分项=做得怎么样、和谁竞争、凭什么赢

    Bonus points: performance, rivals, and edge over them.

  2. 潜台词→你对公司哪些方面感兴趣

    The real question is what draws you to them.

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

表达「了解行业与盈利」

I've looked into the industry and how the company makes money.

表达「产品特点」

Their products stand out because of the after-sales model.

表达「竞对对比」

Compared to their rivals, they're stronger on pricing but weaker on reach.

表达「兴趣点」

What draws me here is their direction in AI-driven products.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：This company is in the automobile industry.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：They operate in the automotive space.

in the automotive space 比 in the industry 更口语。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：What is your profit pattern?
✓ ✓ 地道说法：How does the company make money?

盈利模式别用 profit pattern，用 how they make money。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I am not quite familiar with your company.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I haven't looked into your company as much as I should have.

直接说 not familiar 很生硬，用 haven't looked into 承认但表达改进。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Opportunity always stays for prepared people.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Opportunity favors the prepared.

favors the prepared 是固定表达。
