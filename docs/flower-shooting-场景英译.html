避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Flower are mostly static.
✓ Flowers are mostly static.

主语复数时 be 动词用 are 而非 is

✕ Make the wind visible shape.
✓ Make the shape of the wind visible.

make + 宾语 + 形容词补语的语序要放对

✕ The background behind the flowers is mess.
✓ The background behind the flowers is messy.

messy 是形容词，mess 是名词

✕ Slow the shutter speed and take it.
✓ Slow down the shutter, and there you have it.

slow down 才是调慢；there you have it 是成品示意
