避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：How to prove you are expert?
✓ ✓ 地道说法：How do you prove you're experts?

英文疑问句要用 do you 结构。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The client never asked me this.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The client never asked me this question.

口语中补上 question 更完整。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Commission money is very low.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Commissions are low.

commissions 复数更地道。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Half bottle vinegar is sweating.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The half-baked expert starts sweating.

半瓶醋要意译为 half-baked expert。
