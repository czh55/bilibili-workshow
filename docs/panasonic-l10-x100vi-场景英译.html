避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The camera is very good to control.
✓ The camera handles really well.

「操控性好」英文用 handle well / handling，比 to control 更地道。

✕ It can take photo in high ISO.
✓ The image stays clean and usable even at high ISO.

强调高感下画质表现，用 The image stays clean（画面依然干净）更自然。

✕ I set the focus to the eye of person.
✓ I set the subject-detection target to the eye.

相机菜单语境用 subject detection target（检测目标），比口语说法更准确。

✕ The camera is very small type.
✓ It's not ultra-tiny, but it's a size you'd be happy to carry daily.

small type 是中式英语；用 compact / ultra-tiny 表达体积。
