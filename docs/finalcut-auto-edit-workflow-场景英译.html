避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Expect the AI draft to be the final video.
✓ Treat the draft as a storyboard to be edited over.

The first pass is assembly; taste is the editor's job.

✕ Hand over raw, unlabeled footage.
✓ Organize your footage and list every clip.

The model can only cut what you inventory clearly.

✕ Skip the reference sample.
✓ Provide a reference for pace and style.

Without a reference, the model has no north star for rhythm.

✕ Blame the AI when the cut feels off.
✓ Review the brief and the footage list first.

Most weak cuts come from a weak brief, not a weak model.
