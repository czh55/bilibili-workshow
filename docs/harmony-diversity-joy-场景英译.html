避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Harmonious but different.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Harmony in diversity.

「和而不同」有现成英文习语 harmony in diversity。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The joy in it.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The joy of pursuing it.

乐在其中强调「追求过程」，用 joy of pursuing。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The world is not only me.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The world is not just about me.

不只我一个人→not just about me。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Plain tea and plain rice, I'm also happy.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Even with plain food and simple living, I stay joyful.

粗茶淡饭→plain food and simple living。
