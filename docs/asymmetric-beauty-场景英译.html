The Viewfinder: Keep the Mass Off the Centerline

情境马老师用取景框示范：荷叶形成的绿色块面不能落在画框上下的中线上。块面是画面里最重的视觉元素，居中就形成呆板的一半一半。

一半一半不好看，偏一侧才成立

Half-and-Half Is Ugly — Lean to One Side

情境块面正好落在中线就是一半一半，是最不好看的构图。修正方向：要么天留多一点，要么荷叶留多一点——核心原则就是不要对称。

One Unified Momentum, Broken by a Counter-Leaf

情境花头朝右、叶子朝右、枝也朝右，整个势统一向右走。但旁边一片朝左的反叶四两拨千斤，把势又掰回来——张力由此而生。

The Rule: Balance Within Imbalance

情境中国画最讲究在不平衡当中去求一个平衡，最忌讳四平八稳。这是全片要义，也是可以迁移到任何视觉创作的底层心法。

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Put the flower dead center to make it the star.
✓ Place it at the golden-ratio point.

Centering reads as blunt and artless — the golden point still makes it the star.

✕ Keep everything perfectly symmetrical for a balanced look.
✓ Break the symmetry and lean to one side.

Even split (half-and-half) is exactly what ink painting avoids; balance comes from imbalance.

✕ Point every element in one direction to strengthen the momentum.
✓ Add a small element in the opposite direction to swing it back.

A one-way flow leaks out of the frame; a tiny counter-leaf holds it with a 'light touch'.
