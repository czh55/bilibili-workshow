牛奶与奶缸：两个前置选择

Milk and Pitcher: Two Setup Choices

情境奶泡打发第一关是选料选工具：牛奶首选巴氏杀菌冷藏鲜牛奶，必须冷藏+全脂；奶缸要选打奶缸或全能缸，避免直径最大的拉花缸。

原理：进气与打棉两阶段

The Principle: Aeration Then Texturing

情境奶泡是通过把外部空气搅进牛奶形成的，分为进气与打棉两个阶段：进气让牛奶旋转搅入空气，打棉把大气泡切割成小气泡并均匀分布。

01
中文

奶泡是通过外面的空气搅进去所形成的。

EN

Foam is created by air being folded into the milk.

表达提示fold in（搅入）

02
中文

进气：这个产生的推力，让牛奶旋转起来，把外面的空气搅打进来。

EN

Aeration: the steam's push spins the milk, drawing air in.

表达提示aeration（进气）

03
中文

打棉：通过推力让牛奶旋转、切割，不断形成更小的气泡。

EN

Texturing: the spin and shear keep breaking big bubbles into smaller ones.

表达提示texturing（打棉）

    The big bubbles get chopped into silky micro-bubbles.

实操位置与进气时间

Setup and Steaming Time

情境实操先定缸的位置：缸嘴贴住蒸汽棒、蒸汽棒在三点钟方向。进气时间按机型：家用机3000-5000元档10-15秒（等蒸汽变强再计时），商用机3-5秒。

打棉停点：温度与手感

When to Stop: Temperature and Feel

情境打棉停点靠温度判断：理想约65度。手感法：烫手但拿得住约60度，拿不住70度以上，只有温热约50度。打棉期间切记乱动，防二次进气。

01
中文

我们一般理想的状态是达到一个65度左右的牛奶温度。

EN

The sweet spot is around 65 degrees Celsius.

表达提示sweet spot（理想状态/最佳点）

02
中文

烫手但拿得住，这个温度基本上就在60度上下。

EN

If it's hot but you can still hold it, you're around 60 degrees.

表达提示hot to the touch（烫手）

EN

表达提示re-aeration（二次进气）

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Use whatever milk is in the fridge, room temperature is fine.
✓ Use cold, whole, pasteurized milk.

Room-temp or low-fat milk won't build the structure foam needs.

✕ Steam with the same pitcher you use for pouring art.
✓ Use a steaming pitcher or an all-rounder.

A wide art pitcher barely spins the milk, giving uneven foam.

✕ Start the steaming clock as soon as you open the valve.
✓ Wait for the steam to build and start timing then.

Home machines ramp up slowly; early seconds add no real aeration.

✕ Keep nudging the wand during texturing to even out bubbles.
✓ Hold the wand still once it's set.

Every tweak risks re-aeration, and re-aerated foam is ruined.
