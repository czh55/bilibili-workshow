HR不是筛选期

HR is not a screening gate

情境讲者指出职场中明知你在吹牛却希望你吹得好听的场景就是 HR 面试，HR 不是筛选期而是希望你过关。

There's a situation where people know you're bluffing, but hope you bluff elegantly.

表达提示明知你在吹牛：know you're bluffing

02
中文

HR 这一关不是筛选期，HR 都希望面试者能通过。

EN

The HR round is not a screening gate; HR wants you to pass.

表达提示筛选期：screening gate

03
中文

演戏是要有规则的，演不好就不会被放行。

EN

Acting has its rules; if you act badly, you won't move forward.

表达提示演戏要有规则：acting has its rules

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

    Bluffing isn't lying; it's phrasing things well.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：He hopes you blow beautifully.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：He hopes it sounds good.

blow 在英文口语里有负面歧义，用 sound good 更安全。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I want to try a new challenge.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I'd like to take on a new challenge.

take on a challenge 是固定搭配。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I will lean on the team.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I rely on the strength of my team.

rely on / count on 更自然。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Packaging text is the language art.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：It's the art of packaging your words.

packaging your words 更口语。
