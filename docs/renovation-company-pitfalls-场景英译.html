避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：My renovation cost 40 ten-thousands.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I spent 400,000 on renovating my home.

中文「40万」直译成 ten-thousands 不地道，英文直接用具体数字 400,000。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：More stores means it is more reliable.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：More stores doesn't mean more strength.

「门店多=可靠」直译生硬，用 doesn't mean 的否定句式更口语、更有力。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：They will let you write good reviews.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：They'll nudge you into leaving a five-star review.

「让你写好评」用 nudge someone into doing 更贴切，review 比「好评打分」更自然。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：He only wants to end fast and do the next order.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：All he cares about is wrapping up quickly and moving on.

「尽快结束去干下一单」用 wrap up / move on 表达更地道，order 在装修语境用 job 更合适。
