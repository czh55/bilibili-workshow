✓ ✓ 地道说法：Settle for any job first, then keep hunting—or not.

✓ ✓ 地道说法：You get fired, or you quit out of frustration.

干掉是口语比喻，英文用 get fired / quit。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：My original heart of job hunting.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Why I started job-hunting in the first place.

初心没有直译，用 original intention。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The difference value is big.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：My target and reality are out of sync.

差异大用 out of sync / far apart。
