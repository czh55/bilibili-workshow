要点2：45度侧蹲

Key 2: Squat at 45 Degrees

情境不要正着蹲，正着蹲很显丑。要45度倾斜一点点，然后后面有个靠山往后靠。腿和脚都并拢，乖乖感对比明显。语域：拍照教学，要点讲解。

You must have something behind you to lean on.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Squat like going to the toilet.
✓ It looks like you're using the bathroom.

「拉屎」是夸张口语，英文用 using the bathroom 委婉表达。

✕ Put a backrest behind.
✓ You must have something behind you to lean on.

「靠背」用 something to lean on。

✕ Squat facing forward is ugly.
✓ Don't squat facing forward — it looks really bad.

「正着蹲」用 squat facing forward。

✕ Wear skirt to hide the fat meat.
✓ Wear a little skirt to cover the thigh fullness.

「大腿肉」用 thigh fullness，不说 fat meat。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说蹲只会 squat→用 lean on something（靠背）、angle 45 degrees（45度侧蹲）、well-behaved（乖乖感）
说显丑只会 ugly→用 looks really bad（很显丑）、polished（精致）、demure（乖乖）
说挡肉只会 hide→用 cover the fullness（挡肉）、camera side（镜头侧）、props（道具）
