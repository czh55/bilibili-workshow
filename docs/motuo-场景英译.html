避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The road took 48 years to build.
✓ It took forty-eight years to build this road.

took 是动词核心，主语是 it/people，不是 the road；the road took ... 是把 took 误当「花费」主宾颠倒的直译腔。

✕ I want to go to Motuo.
✓ I want to make it to Motuo.

make it to 传达「历经艰险终于到达」，比 go to 更贴合这趟旅程的语气。

✕ The tire pattern is gone.
✓ The tread is completely worn down.

胎纹的专业说法是 tread（胎面纹路），tire pattern 是花纹图案的意思；worn down 比 gone 更地道。

✕ The mountain is 7000 meters high.
✓ There's a drop of nearly seven thousand meters.

讲两地落差用 a drop of / an elevation difference of，直接说 mountain is high 无法表达「相对落差」。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

只会用 go to 表达「去某地」→按语境选 make it to（终于到达）、head to（前往）、set off for（出发去）
说海拔只会 altitude→区分 altitude（海拔）、elevation（高程）、a drop of（落差）、at the pass（垭口处）
说风景好只会 beautiful→用 spectacular（壮观的）、silvery-white（银白）、fold into（折叠进）、stunning（绝美）更精准
