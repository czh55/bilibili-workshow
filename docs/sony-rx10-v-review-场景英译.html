避坑 PITFALLS

✕ It covers 24mm to 600mm focus length.
✓ It covers 24 to 600 millimeters in focal length.

焦段是 focal length（焦距），不是 focus length；focus 是「对焦」，语义完全不同。

✕ The camera is not too big.
✓ The camera is heavier than it looks.

「不轻」用 heavier / not that light；not too big 表达的是体积大小，不是重量。

✕ The sensor is one inch big.
✓ The sensor is one inch — it isn't big enough.

one inch big 中式直译；直接用 one inch 作表语即可。

✕ The camera's focus is very fast.
✓ The camera's autofocus is a huge improvement.

对焦性能用 autofocus（自动对焦）；focus 也可用但 autofocus 更贴合相机语境。
