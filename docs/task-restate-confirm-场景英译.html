避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Say good is not enough.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：A mere 'OK' is not enough.

mere 强调「只是」更地道。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The boss's pot is not easy to throw over.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The blame is hard to pin on you.

pin the blame 是固定表达。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Print the contract two shares.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Print two copies of the contract.

一式两份 = two copies。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Let boss all in the palm.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Keep your boss fully in control.

keep ... in control 更自然。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

收到指令就动手→先复述确认再动手
把复述当作「不信任」→把复述当作「负责与尊重」
