避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The car costs 1700万.
✓ Each car costs around 17 million dollars.

中文「1700万」不要直译；英文用 17 million 或 $17M。

✕ The wing gives down force.
✓ The inverted wings generate downforce.

downforce（下压力）是一个专业合成词，写成一个单词；give 太口语，用 generate/produce。

✕ F1 can drive upside-down on the tunnel top.
✓ Given a tunnel long enough, an F1 car could drive upside down on the ceiling.

tunnel top 中式表达；用 on the ceiling 更自然，且「倒着开」用 drive upside down。

✕ The city must pay money to F1.
✓ Cities pay F1 a hosting fee.

pay money to 冗余；直接说 pay a hosting fee（付举办费）。
