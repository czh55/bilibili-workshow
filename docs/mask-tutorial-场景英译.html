避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The mask is very important in photos and videos.
✓ The mask is really important in both photo editing and video editing.

photo editing 才是「修图」，photo 本身只是照片

✕ Paint white where do you want to show.
✓ Paint white where you want to show.

where 引导从句时用陈述语序，不用疑问倒装

✕ I want to adjust only at face.
✓ I want the adjustment to apply only to the face.

apply to 才表示「作用于」，介词不能漏

✕ Slide the mask to left.
✓ Slide the mask to the left.

固定说法 to the left / to the right 需加 the
