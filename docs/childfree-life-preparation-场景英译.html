避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：There is no medicine for regret in this world.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：There's no undo button in life.

undo button 是「后悔药」的常用口语说法。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：My anti-risk ability is low.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：My ability to absorb risk is lower.

absorb risk 比 anti-risk ability 地道。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Marriage is an economic partner system.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Marriage is a form of economic partnership.

partnership 表达合伙人制更自然。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：When I have a headache and fever.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：When I'm under the weather.

under the weather 是「身体不适」的固定表达。
