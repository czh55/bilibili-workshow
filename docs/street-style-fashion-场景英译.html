今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

正面平拍有什么问题？

It looks flat and boring — better to shoot from the side.

如何让构图一分为二？

Put the ground line into the bottom of the frame.

半身构图头应该放哪里？

Place the top of the head near the upper-right corner.

怎么拍出松弛感？

Give the subject a natural action, like looking to the side, and avoid the lens.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Shoot everything head-on.
✓ Move to the subject's side and raise the camera.

Head-on shots look flat; an angle adds depth and style.

✕ Crop the head tightly.
✓ Leave a little headroom but not too much.

No headroom feels cramped; too much looks empty.

✕ Keep whatever is in the background.
✓ Look for a clean backdrop and reposition.

Clutter like drains or white walls ruins the frame.

✕ Ask the subject to smile on cue.
✓ Give 2-3 concrete pose cues instead.

Specific cues like 'tilt your shoulder' produce natural looks.

以前正面直拍对着镜头→现在侧位站位、机位举高
