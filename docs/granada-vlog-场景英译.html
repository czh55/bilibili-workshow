避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Flamenco is very beautiful dance.
✓ Flamenco was born out of despair.

介绍艺术形式起源比泛泛夸好看更有信息量；born out of despair 是历史性表达。

✕ The palace cost a lot of money to get.
✓ The Alhambra tickets are incredibly hard to get.

tickets hard to get（票难抢）与 palace costs money（宫殿造价）是完全不同的意思，别混。

✕ I want to take photos with you.
✓ Can we take a photo together?

请求合影的礼貌说法是 Can we take a photo together? / Would you mind a photo?

✕ The old town has no parking.
✓ The hotel's website promised parking, but there was none.

把问题说具体：是酒店承诺有车位实际没有，而不是古镇本身没停车场。
