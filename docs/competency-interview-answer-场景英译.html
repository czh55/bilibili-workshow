避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I think I can do this job well.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I'm confident I can deliver strong results in this role.

deliver results 比 do the job well 更有职场质感。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：My ability is very strong.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：My track record shows consistent, measurable outcomes.

用 track record 代替抽象的能力自夸。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I completed contract amount 40 million.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I closed contracts totaling over 40 million.

contracts totaling 是描述合同金额的地道表达。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I will restate my viewpoint again.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Let me close by restating my point.

close by restating 表达收尾重申。
