避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Between knowledge and action there's a thing called scene.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Between knowing and doing, there's something called the scenario.

scene 指风景/画面，此处表「情境」用 scenario 或 situation 更贴切。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Overcome a lazy word and you can solve it.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Getting past laziness is all it takes.

「克服一个懒字」直译成 lazy word 是中式表达，英文里直接说 laziness。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The house certificate is collected away by the bank.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The bank seizes the mortgaged deed.

银行收回抵押物用 seize / repossess，collect 不地道。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Your performance under adversity distinguishes you from all beings.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：How you perform under adversity sets you apart from the crowd.

all beings 有宗教腔，从人群中区分开用 set apart from the crowd。
