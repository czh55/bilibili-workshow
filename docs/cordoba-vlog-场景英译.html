今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

介绍科尔多瓦清真寺的独特之处

It's the only cathedral in the world built inside a mosque, not replacing it.

描述里面的风格对比

Inside, you see Islamic style on one side and a dazzling Catholic cathedral on the other.

吐槽被地图骗的经历

The map said it was open, but when we got there, they were closed.

介绍宫廷城被遗忘的历史

The palace city was destroyed in a civil war and forgotten for centuries.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The mosque has a church in it.
✓ There's a cathedral built inside the mosque.

built inside（嵌入）比 has a church in it 更准确，强调建筑关系而非包含。

✕ The cathedral took the place of the mosque.
✓ The cathedral was built into the mosque, not replacing it.

科尔多瓦的特殊性是「嵌入不取代」，直接用取代句式会与史实相反。

✕ It was very flourish.
✓ It was the center of the Islamic world.

flourish 是动词，不是形容词；说繁华用 prosperous / thriving / the center of ...。

✕ The city is 1000 years old.
✓ The city was the Islamic world's center in the tenth century.

讲历史地位比讲年数更精确；具体到世纪 the tenth century。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说融合只会 mix→用 fusion（融合）、jarringly different（割裂反差）、blend of styles
说繁华只会 very rich→用 hundreds of thousands of people、thriving、the center of the world
说建筑只会 old building→用 cathedral、mosque、columns（廊柱）、palace city（宫廷城）
