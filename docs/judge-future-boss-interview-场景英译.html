好领导是导师

A Good Boss Is a Mentor

情境讲者指出工作没劲、看不到成长可能是因为跟错了领导，好领导是导师。

01
中文

工作越干越没劲，越干越看不到自己的成长，因为你跟错了领导。

EN

If work gets more draining and you see no growth, you've probably picked the wrong boss.

表达提示跟错了领导：picked the wrong boss

02
中文

好领导就是一位好导师，会身体力行地去教你去引导你。

EN

A good boss is a mentor who teaches and guides you by example.

表达提示身体力行：by example

03
中文

好的领导既会磨砺你，恰当的时候也会提拔你。

EN

A good leader challenges you, but promotes you when the time is right.

表达提示磨砺你：challenge you

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 成长停滞 → 先检查领导

    When growth stalls, check the leadership first.

  2. 好领导 = 磨砺 + 提拔

    A good boss both sharpens and elevates you.

尊重与专业

Respect and Expertise

情境讲者给出前两个评估维度：尊重看面试体验，专业看观点深度。

01
中文

好的领导在面试中会给到你一个相对良好的面试体验。

EN

A good boss gives you a decent interview experience.

表达提示面试体验：interview experience

02
中文

你要评估一下面试官的观点或解答是不是具备专业性。

EN

You should assess whether the interviewer's views and answers are truly professional.

表达提示具备专业性：truly professional

03
中文

如果面试官的专业水平还不如你，未来你的成长会非常有限。

EN

If the interviewer is less professional than you, your future growth will be very limited.

表达提示成长有限：growth will be limited

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 尊重 = 态度的温度计

    Respect is the thermometer of attitude.

  2. 专业 = 能力的水位线

    Expertise sets the ceiling for how much you'll learn.

开放与激情

Openness and Passion

01
中文

好的领导面对合理建议，是很愿意去接纳的。

EN

A good leader is willing to take on reasonable suggestions.

表达提示接纳建议：take on suggestions

02
中文

如果面试官一味地否定甚至打击你，被PUA的概率非常高。

EN

If the interviewer keeps rejecting or belittling you, you're very likely to be gaslighted later.

表达提示PUA：gaslight

03
中文

EN

When a good leader talks about their business and team, their eyes light up.

    Openness decides how you two will work together.

  2. 激情 → 决定团队氛围

    Passion reveals the energy of the team.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：He points me out of problems.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：He challenges me and helps me grow.

磨砺不是指出问题，用 challenge / push 表达。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：His eyes are letting out light.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：His eyes light up.

眼睛放光是 eyes light up，不要直译。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：He always hits you in the interview.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：He keeps rejecting or belittling you.

打击用 belittle / put down 表达。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The experience of interview is good.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：He gave me a decent interview experience.

decent 口语自然。
