避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The bridge is 2000 years old.
✓ Half the bridge dates back two thousand years; the other half is sixteenth century.

只说不全是2000年，分开说明两半年代才准确。

✕ I will not fail my class.
✓ You won't fail any class this semester.

这是传说/习俗，用 legend says + 将来时更有叙述感；fail any class 比 my class 准确。

✕ The university is very old.
✓ It's one of the oldest universities in the world.

具体到 one of the oldest in the world 更有信息量。

✕ The police can't catch students.
✓ The police couldn't arrest students for crimes in the Middle Ages.

补充时间状语 in the Middle Ages 使历史陈述更严谨。
