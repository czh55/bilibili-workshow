额头决定上庭与侧脸的衔接

The Forehead Bridges the Upper Face and Profile

情境额头是上庭的主体，它决定从发际线到眉骨的过渡是否流畅，也影响侧脸从眉骨到鼻根的整体节奏。

三个设计维度：高度、弧度、平面

Three Dimensions: Height, Curve, and Plane

情境额头设计看三个维度：高度（上下占比）、弧度（正面的圆润与侧面的平直）、平面（眉骨上方是否有自然的过渡台阶）。

01
中文

EN

表达提示proportion（比例）

02
中文

EN

表达提示youthful（幼态）

03
中文

眉骨上方的过渡，要有一个自然的台阶。

EN

表达提示natural step（自然台阶）

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Design the forehead in isolation.
✓ Treat it as the bridge between upper face and profile.

A forehead only works in context with hairline, brow, and nose.

✕ Make the forehead as round as possible.
✓ Balance the curve between youthful and hard.

Extreme curves tip the face into a caricature.

✕ Ignore the transition at the brow bone.
✓ Design a natural step above the brow.

A flat seam above the brow ruins the profile flow.

✕ Add height without checking proportion.
✓ Let the face thirds decide the height.

Height without proportion throws the whole face off.

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

把额头当独立部位→作为上庭与侧脸衔接的枢纽设计
只看圆润度→从高度-弧度-平面三维评估
忽视眉骨过渡→关注自然台阶与整体节奏
