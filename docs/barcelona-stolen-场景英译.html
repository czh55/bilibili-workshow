开场：所有东西都在这里被偷了

Opening: Everything Was Stolen Here

情境博主以遗憾开场：今天没有高质量视频和充满能量的博主，因为所有东西都在巴塞罗那被偷了。语域：自嘲式口播。

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The car is burning on fire.
✓ Your car is on fire.

on fire 已是「着火」，burning on fire 重复冗余。

✕ He stole my bag, I was very angry.
✓ It hit me: where's my bag?

表达「突然反应过来」用 it hit me / it dawned on me，比 I was very angry 更有画面。

✕ I can't file report.
✓ I couldn't file a report because my tire was flat.

file a report（报案）是固定搭配，不能漏 a；补充原因更完整。

✕ Gaudí builded the church.
✓ Gaudí designed the cathedral with tree-like columns.

build 的过去式是 built 不是 builded；且用 designed 更准确。
