避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：House prices fell to the sky.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Prices would have gone through the roof.

through the roof 是「涨上天」的地道说法。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The safe cushion of big cities is the thickest.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Big cities offer the thickest safety cushion.

用 offer 描述城市提供安全垫，表达更自然。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Use a small money to chase hot spots.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Take a small slice of money to chase what's hot.

a slice of money 比 a small money 自然。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Buy the house you should buy, buy the insurance you should buy.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Buy the insurance you need, buy the home if it makes sense.

重复句式要拆开变化，if it makes sense 更口语。
