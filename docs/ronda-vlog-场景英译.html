避坑 PITFALLS

✕ I very happy to see you again.
✓ Nice to meet you again / Great to see you again.

I very happy 缺少 be 动词；地道问候用 Nice/Great to see you again。

✕ The bull is killed by force.
✓ The bull is sometimes pardoned.

赦免用 pardon/spare，不是「被迫」；语义相反。

✕ Ronda is a city on the cliff.
✓ Ronda is perched on a cliff.

perched on 比 on 更能表达「建在悬崖边缘」的悬空感。

✕ The bridge in the middle has a room.
✓ In the middle of the bridge is a small stone chamber.

倒装句 In the middle ... is ... 是更自然的描述句式。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说历史只会 long time ago→用 in the early days、from this very bullring、it turned out that ...（意外发现）
说概率只会 maybe→用 there's a very small chance、highly unlikely、rarely
说适合只会 suitable→用 perfect for eloping、ideal for、the most suitable town
