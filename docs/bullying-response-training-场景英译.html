避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Teach the child to fight back.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Teach the child to stand up for themselves.

打回去不一定用 fight back，stand up for oneself 更贴「应对」本意。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The dad is absent in family education.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Dad is absent from parenting.

家庭教育→parenting，更口语。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Dogs train their survival skills when fighting.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Dogs wrestle — it's survival training, not fighting.

wrestle 才是狗狗打闹的地道词。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：There will be an automated response to beat someone back.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：It becomes an automatic, defensive reflex.

自动化反应→a defensive reflex。
