避坑 PITFALLS

✕ He is spoiled by German sausages to me.
✓ I'm spoiled by the sausages in Germany.

be spoiled by（被…惯坏）是固定搭配，不要加 to me。

✕ The place is busy for a reason why.
✓ The place is busy for a reason.

busy for a reason 已是完整表达，不要画蛇添足加 why。

✕ The meat is very soft and salt.
✓ The meat is soft, salty, and very tender.

形容词链用 salty（咸的），salt 是名词。

✕ I leave company to do my things.
✓ I've decided to leave the company and do my own thing.

离职用 leave the company；「做自己的事业」是 do my own thing。
