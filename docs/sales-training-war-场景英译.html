外部专家的现实

The reality of external experts

情境讲者评点请外部专家的问题，点破非同行专家只是混口饭吃，劝大家别为难他，毕竟谁都不肯把看家本领掏出来。

01
中文

不管是内部高手还是请外部专家来讲，如果没有设计过，效果都不会很好。

Whether you bring in your own top salespeople or an outside expert, without careful design the results won't be much good.

表达提示精心设计：carefully designed

With the economy slowing and every company slashing budgets, the fact that you still hire outside experts shows you're a company with real resources.

表达提示砍预算：slash budgets

If the outside expert isn't from your industry, just take it with a grain of salt — they're only earning a living, so don't make things hard for them.

攻守部队的原理

The principle behind the method

情境讲者讲原理「充分发挥学员自主性」，并分享请三个互相不买账高手的关键经验，故意埋下悬念。

01
中文

我从一本书里学来的原理，原文是「充分发挥学员的自主性」，翻译成人话就是「挑动群众斗群众」。

EN

The principle I picked up from a book reads: fully unleash the trainees' initiative — in plain words, set the crowd against the crowd.

表达提示充分发挥学员的自主性：fully unleash trainees' initiative

02
中文

这里有一个经验：不要请一个高手，要请三个，而且要请三个互相不买账的高手。

EN

Here's the key trick: don't invite just one expert — invite three, and make sure they don't see eye to eye with one another.

表达提示互相不买账：don't see eye to eye

03
中文

彼此买账的你反而不要请，为什么？待会你就明白了。

EN

Experts who get along with each other? Don't invite them. Why? You'll understand in a moment.

表达提示待会你就明白了：you'll understand in a moment

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 书里的原理「发挥自主性」 → 让学员互相斗起来

    The book's principle of autonomy means letting trainees compete against one another.

  2. 请三个互相不买账的高手 → 故意制造对立，让火花自然烧起来

    Three rival experts are invited on purpose — the friction does the teaching.
S301:05–01:49

攻守部队玩法

How the game works

情境讲者详细演示分组、挂白板、进攻挑漏洞、防守限时对策的玩法，并教大家录音留档、煽风点火。

01
中文

上课时把所有人分成 ABC 三组，每组发一个销售痛点问题，三位高手各带一组，把解决方案写在白板纸上挂起来。

EN

In class, split everyone into groups A, B and C, give each group a sales pain point, let each expert lead one group, and post their solutions on flipchart paper.

表达提示销售痛点：sales pain point；白板纸：flipchart paper

02
中文

两个组当进攻小组，去挑防守组写在白板纸上的漏洞，每挑出一个漏洞，就相当于揍了对方一拳。

EN

Two groups attack by picking holes in the defending group's flipchart — every hole they find counts as a punch landed on the opponent.

表达提示挑漏洞：pick holes in；揍一拳：land a punch

03
中文

防守组要在五秒内想出对策，想不出来就判对方得一分；被揍得差不多了就喊停，换下一组继续打。

EN

The defending group has five seconds to come up with a counter, or the attackers score a point; when they've taken enough hits, call time and rotate in the next group.

表达提示想对策：come up with a counter；喊停：call time

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 进攻 = 挑对方方案里的漏洞

    Attacking means hunting for holes in the opposing plan.

  2. 限时五秒 = 逼出临场应变

    The five-second limit forces quick, on-the-spot responses.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：He can't pour the dumplings out of the teapot.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：He knows his stuff but just can't put it into words.

「茶壶里倒饺子——有货倒不出」是中文俗语，不可字面直译；可用 know his stuff but can't explain it / be brilliant yet unable to teach。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Rile the masses to fight the masses.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Set one team against another and let them battle it out.

「挑动群众斗群众」借自老话，此处调侃式表达「让学员互相较劲」；英文用 set the crowd against the crowd 或 get them to battle it out 更自然。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Invite three experts who don't buy each other.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Invite three experts who don't see eye to eye.

「互相不买账」= 互不服气、互不认可；buy 虽有「相信」义，但 don't buy each other 不用于人，惯用 see eye to eye。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：They just eat a bowl of rice for a living.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：They're only earning a living / just in it for the paycheck.

「混口饭吃」不宜字面翻译，用 earn a living 或 be in it for the paycheck 更地道。
