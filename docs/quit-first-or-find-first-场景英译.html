The Dilemma of Quitting First

表达提示改简历求职：polish a resume and job-hunt

Balancing and the Exception

情境讲者给出忙碌的解法与放手一搏的例外条件。

02

03

    Prioritize and make trade-offs to balance work and job-hunting.

  2. 例外才放手 → 先看影响

    Only go all in when the job truly blocks your search.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Quit first and then look for a job.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Don't resign blindly—hunt while you're still employed.

裸辞是 resign blindly / quit without a plan。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I have enough bottom energy.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Being employed gives me more leverage.

底气是 leverage，不是 bottom energy。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：No security and no sense of safety.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：No safety net, and that's scary.

安全感用 safety net，更具体。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Let go and fight.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：It's okay to go all in and focus on the search.

放手一搏用 go all in。
