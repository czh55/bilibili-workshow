今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

新手拍高反光珠宝最容易犯什么错？

Using a softbox directly, which leaves metal pitch black.

为什么加灯解决不了死黑？

Because the problem is light direction and softening, not quantity.

专业解法的三个动作是什么？

Turn off the side light, move the key light behind, and use tracing paper as a canopy.

内侧死黑怎么解决？

Put a silver card on each side to bounce light in.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Add more lights when metal goes black.
✓ Reduce lights and rethink direction.

More lamps with the wrong direction make reflections worse.

✕ Shoot glossy metal with a bare softbox.
✓ Diffuse through tracing paper or a canopy.

Direct hard light creates black-out on reflective surfaces.

✕ Ignore the leftover inner shadow.
✓ Use silver cards to fill the shadow area.

A key light fixes the big areas; small cards fix the corners.

✕ Move lights around randomly.
✓ Follow the order: side off, key behind, canopy, cards.

A clear sequence beats trial-and-error.
