中文

03
中文

    Leave an exit both sides can accept.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：HR holds me very tightly.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：HR has you on a leash.

被拿捏→have someone on a leash。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：12 salaries or 14 salaries?
✓ ✓ 地道说法：12 or 14 monthly paychecks?

12薪→12 monthly paychecks / payments。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Turn over the win.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Turn the tables.

逆风翻盘→turn the tables。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The probation salary is not lower than 80%.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The trial wage at no less than 80%.

试用期→trial period / probation；不低于→no less than。
