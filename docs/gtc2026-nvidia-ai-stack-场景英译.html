避坑 PITFALLS

✕ NVIDIA has very high profit.
✓ NVIDIA enjoys sky-high gross margins.

profit 与 margin 不同：gross margin（毛利率）才是财经语境的标准说法；very high 太弱，用 sky-high。

✕ The customer wants to build himself.
✓ The customers want to do it themselves / go in-house.

build himself 中式；go in-house（自建）或 do it themselves 更地道。

✕ The GPU price is 10 times lower.
✓ Inference cost dropped to one-tenth.

「降到十分之一」是 drop to one-tenth；price is 10 times lower 有歧义（可能理解为便宜10倍=不存在的说法）。

✕ They buy the switch and protocol too.
✓ The switch chips, protocols, and backplanes come bundled.

buy too 口语冗余；come bundled / full package 才是「全套打包」的自然说法。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说赚钱只会 make money→用 print money（印钞）、sky-high margins（超高毛利）、revenue juggernaut（营收怪兽）
