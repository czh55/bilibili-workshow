避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The hotel is builded in 1893.
✓ The hotel was built in 1893.

build 的过去分词是 built，不是 builded；被动语态用 was/were + built。

✕ I want to relax in Cam Ranh Bay.
✓ I was torn between relaxing and seeing the villas.

be torn between A and B 表达「两难、纠结」，比单纯的 want to 更有画面感。

✕ The lake is name after a poet.
✓ The lake is named after the famous poet Ho Xuan Huong.

be named after 固定搭配，name 必须用过去分词 named。

✕ The railway was abandoned because war.
✓ The railway was abandoned because of the war.

because of + 名词；because 后要跟完整的句子。
