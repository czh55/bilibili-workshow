今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

新官上任别急着做什么？

Don't rush into one-on-one talks with the team.

向上要哪三样？

A map, a key (authority), and a blacklist (power structure).

授权要问清楚什么？

Which decisions I own and which I escalate.

立威靠什么？

Results, not repeating "the boss says".
