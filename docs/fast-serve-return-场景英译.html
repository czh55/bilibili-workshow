Pitfall & Wrap-Up: Stay Low, Swing Through

情境反面示范：过早过短收拍、重心过早向上，回球质量低、容易失误。正确做法是压住重心、充分挥长，像扫倒一排障碍物一样完整送拍。

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Swing harder to handle a fast serve.
✓ Keep the take-back small and step into the ball.

Big swings need time you simply don't have against a fast serve.

✕ Cut the follow-through short to recover faster.
✓ Follow through fully, well past your body.

A short finish kills the ball's stability and drives up errors.

✕ Rise up early to gain power.
✓ Stay low through contact.

Lifting your weight early destroys control on the return.

✕ Think of the return as just swinging at the ball.
✓ Think of it as sweeping through a row of obstacles.

The sweep image keeps the swing long, smooth, and continuous.
