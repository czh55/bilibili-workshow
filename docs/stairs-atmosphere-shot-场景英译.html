今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

如何拍出楼梯的纵深感？

Raise the phone, step back, and shoot at 1.5x.

俯拍裙摆有什么效果？

It adds a dynamic, cinematic feel.

人物在画面哪里最出片？

Place the subject on the rule-of-thirds line.

怎么避免显腿短？

Shoot from the side at a raised angle, not head-on and low.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Crouch low for a flat shot.
✓ Raise the phone and shoot from a new angle.

Low flat shots usually flop and shorten the legs.

✕ Zoom all the way in.
✓ Use 1.5x or 2x only.

Heavy digital zoom hurts image quality in low light.

✕ Stand anywhere in the frame.
✓ Follow the middle-lower two cells or the thirds line.

Deliberate placement makes the composition work.

✕ Face the camera head-on.
✓ Turn to the side and avoid the lens.

A side stance plus hand-back pose creates a triangle structure.
