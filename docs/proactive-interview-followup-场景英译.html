被动等待的代价

The Cost of Waiting

情境讲者指出被动等待会让好机会越来越远，很多求职者因怕掉价而不敢主动问。

01
中文

心仪的公司迟迟没有给面试反馈，你就一定不能一味等着。

EN

If your dream company keeps you waiting for feedback, don't just sit around.

表达提示心仪的公司：dream company

02
中文

越被动等待，好机会离你会越远。

EN

The more passively you wait, the further great opportunities drift away.

表达提示被动等待：wait passively

03
中文

好工作一定不是等来的，需要我们主动出击。

EN

Good jobs are never won by waiting—you have to go after them.

表达提示主动出击：go after them

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 等 = 把节奏交给对方

    Waiting hands the pace over to them.

  2. 矜持 → 机会溜走

    Being too reserved lets opportunities slip away.

面试前中段主动

Being Proactive During the Interview

情境讲者给出前两个主动方法：表明准备状态与面试结束时的致谢和争取。

01
中文

主动向面试官表明你的准备状态，让他感受到你很重视这场面试。

EN

Proactively show the interviewer how prepared you are, so they can feel you really value this interview.

表达提示准备状态：how prepared you are

02
中文

面试快结束的时候，一定要主动感谢面试官。

EN

As the interview wraps up, be sure to thank the interviewer sincerely.

表达提示主动感谢：thank them sincerely

03
中文

互动不错时，抓住机会询问面试官，方便加一下他的联系方式。

EN

If the interaction went well, seize the moment and ask for the interviewer's contact info.

表达提示联系方式：contact info

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 主动 = 让对方感知到重视

    Being proactive means making your commitment visible.

  2. 好互动 → 顺势争取联系

    When rapport is good, naturally ask for a way to stay in touch.
S300:45-01:06

面试后跟进

Following Up After the Interview

情境讲者说明面试后两三天要主动问结果，主动追问是在传递重视。

01
中文

面试结束之后的两三天，如果还没收到反馈，一定要主动去问结果。

EN

Two or three days after the interview, if you still haven't heard back, go ahead and ask.

表达提示主动去问结果：go ahead and ask

02
中文

不要觉得主动去问会掉价，与机会失之交臂才是最大的损失。

EN

Don't worry that asking will make you look cheap—losing the opportunity is the real loss.

表达提示失之交臂：lose the opportunity

03
中文

主动追问结果，就是在告诉对方我很重视这个机会。

EN

Following up is your way of saying this opportunity really matters to me.

表达提示主动追问：following up

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 追问 ≠ 掉价 → 传递重视

    Following up isn't losing face; it's signaling commitment.

  2. 机会损失 > 面子损失

    Losing the opportunity costs more than any embarrassment.

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

表达「希望尽快有反馈」

I'd really appreciate it if I could get feedback soon.

表达「加联系方式」

Would you mind if I got your contact info?

表达「主动问结果」

I was wondering if you have an update on the interview result.

表达「我很重视这个机会」

This opportunity really matters to me, so I wanted to check in.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I want to take the initiative to ask.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I wanted to check in on the result.

check in 比 take the initiative to ask 自然得多。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Will asking make my value lower?
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Will following up make me look desperate?

掉价用 look desperate / lose face 表达。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Add his contact method.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Ask for his contact info.

联系方式是 contact info / contact details。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The chance passed by with me.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The opportunity slipped away.

失之交臂用 slip away 表达。
