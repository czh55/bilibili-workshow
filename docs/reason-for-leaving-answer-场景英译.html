    What's really being tested is stability and resilience.

思路一：不接招

Approach One: Skip the Frame

情境讲者给出第一种答法：不顺着问题，另起思路表明长期追随的决心。

01
中文

思路一，不顺着面试官的问题，另起一个思路。

EN

Approach one: don't follow the interviewer's framing—pivot to your own point.

表达提示另起思路：pivot to your own point

02
中文

表明自己做好了长期追随公司发展的准备。

EN

Make it clear you're ready to grow with the company long term.

表达提示长期追随：grow with the company long term

03
中文

遇到困难会去解决问题，不会以离职逃避。

EN

When challenges come, you solve them instead of quitting to escape.

表达提示以离职逃避：quit to escape

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 不答「为什么走」 → 答「我会留下」

    Don't explain why you'd leave—explain why you'll stay.

  2. 表态要靠实例支撑

    Back up the commitment with a real example.

思路二：顺着答

Approach Two: Answer Within the Frame

情境讲者给出第二种答法：顺着问题回答，把离职归结为职业规划冲突。

01
中文

思路二，顺着面试官的问题去回答。

EN

Approach two: answer within the interviewer's frame.

表达提示顺着问题：within the interviewer's frame

02
中文

表达提示职业规划清晰：a clear career plan

03
中文

最大的离职可能性，是当时的发展与职业规划相互冲突。

EN

If I ever left, it would be because the role and my career plan diverged.

表达提示相互冲突：diverge

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 把离职原因 → 归结为规划错位

    Frame leaving as a mismatch between the role and your plan.

  2. 诚实 + 有规划感

    It sounds honest and shows you think ahead.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The biggest reason I leave is the plan conflicts.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：If I ever left, it would be because the role and my plan diverged.

假设句式 if I ever left 更自然，避免绝对化。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I will not use resignation to escape.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I solve problems rather than quit to escape them.

escape 用 quit to escape 组合，别直译。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Congratulations to you.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Congratulations are in order.

恭喜你用 congratulations are in order 更地道。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I am stable and can bear pressure.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I'm dependable and I handle pressure well.

稳定与抗压性用 dependable / handle pressure 表达。
