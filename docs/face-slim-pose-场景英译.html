教显脸小的基础姿势

Tilt 15 degrees toward the lens and sweep your jawline with one hand.

说杯子挡脸的正确拍法

Rest one hand flat on the table and reach the other forward holding the cup.

教撩发收尾

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ I prop my face on my hand to look slimmer.
✓ Prop your face and it looks wider — tilt 15 degrees toward the lens instead.

撑脸反而显脸大，正确做法是斜向镜头加手部层次。

✕ I hold the cup against my face.
✓ Don't hold the cup against your face — rest one hand flat and reach forward with the other.

杯子挡脸是「前伸制造景深」，不是贴脸遮挡。

✕ I rest my chin directly on my palm.
✓ Rest on the back of your hand and look down at your jaw.

手撑脸用反手姿势，视线向下更显脸小。

✕ I reach my hand straight at the camera.
✓ Reach forward at an angle for depth — a straight reach looks flat.

手前伸要有角度和特写，直直伸手缺乏层次。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

只会说 hold the cup→用 rest one hand flat（一手平放）、reach forward（前伸）、to the side of your body（向身侧）细分动作
只会说 move my hair→用 toss your hair back（向后撩）、flip your hair（撩头发）
