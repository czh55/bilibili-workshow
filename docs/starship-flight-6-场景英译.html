今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

形容火箭升空现场

The noise of it going up was insane — I've never seen anything like it.

解释为什么落入海里

描述再入大气层

分享现场激动感受

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The rocket's noise is very big.
✓ The noise of it going up was insane.

very big 口语无力；用 insane / deafening / overwhelming 形容现场震撼。

✕ It can get back from the sea?
✓ Can they recover it from the sea?

can't get back 是被动回收，英文用 recover（回收）更准确。

✕ Going to Mars takes 6 months at max payload.
✓ If you want to maximize payload, it takes six months.

at max payload 中式；用 to maximize payload（要最大化载荷）表达条件。

✕ I got goose pimples.
✓ I got chills / goosebumps.

英文表示「起鸡皮疙瘩」用 get chills 或 get goosebumps，不是 goose pimples（中式翻译）。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

形容震撼只会 amazing→用 insane（离谱）、epic（史诗级）、chills down my spine（脊梁发冷）
说风险大只会 dangerous→用 greater risks（更大风险）、commanded to land out to sea（被迫海上着陆）
说改变了看法只会 changed me→用 changed my entire perspective（改变整个人生视角）、reframed how I see things（重塑认知）
