Beans: Dark Roast and Fresh

情境拉花豆子选深烘焙、新鲜的。深烘焙油脂丰富、线条稳定、不易晕染；浅烘焙拉花困难、对比度差。

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Use light-roasted beans for latte art.
✓ Choose a dark roast, as fresh as possible.

Light roasts bleed and blur — rich crema holds the line.

✕ Pull the shot first, then froth the milk.
✓ Prepare espresso and milk at the same time.

Frothing after the shot lets the crema sit and the milk wait, hurting the pour.

✕ Set the same milk temperature every time.
✓ Adjust by crema quality and design complexity.

Easy crema/simple designs take higher heat; tricky ones take lower.

✕ Chat before pouring after frothing.
✓ Pour immediately once the milk is ready.

Sitting milk separates and the foam goes coarse.
