避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Be natural is the most not natural.
✓ The most unnatural direction of all is 'just be natural'.

「最不自然的指引」用 the most unnatural direction，不要逐字翻译。

✕ The pose is not made, is moved.
✓ A pose isn't struck — it's moved into.

「摆姿势」用 strike a pose；「动出来」用 move into。

✕ Use the live model.
✓ Use your phone's Live mode.

「实况模式」是 Live mode / Live Photo，不是 live model。

✕ There is always one OK.
✓ No matter what, you'll get at least one good one.

「总会有一张可以」用 at least one good one。
