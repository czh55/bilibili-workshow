Bias and Concerns About Commitment

01

03
中文

  2. 担忧 = 投入与回报的权衡

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I can't reach high and won't lower myself.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I'm stuck between roles I don't want and roles I can't get.

高不成低不就直译拗口，用 stuck between 表达卡住的处境。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：My way is wrong, and my effort is in vain.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Wrong methods just waste your effort.

in vain 偏书面，口语用 waste your effort。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Lower your job expectation.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Be open to a lateral move or a pay cut.

具体化「降低预期」才符合求职语境。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：Polish your resume thickly.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：Make your experience depth stand out.

打磨简历是让亮点凸显，直译 thickness 不成立。
