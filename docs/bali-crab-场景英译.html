避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The space is open 24 hours all over the world.
✓ The coworking space is open 24 hours, with remote workers from all over the world.

all over the world 修饰的是「来自哪里的人」，不能用来形容空间的开放时间。

✕ I want to press a pause key.
✓ I want to hit pause.

「按暂停键」英文惯用 hit pause / press pause，不逐字说 pause key。

✕ The flowers correspond different gods.
✓ Different flowers correspond to the guardian deities.

correspond to 是固定搭配，介词 to 不能省略。

✕ The beach is famous by sunset.
✓ The beach is famous for its stunning sunsets.

be famous for（因…而闻名）；by 表达方式/手段，语义不对。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

说很忙只会 busy→用 get completely packed（塞满）、jam-packed（爆满）、run from thing to thing
说风景好只会 beautiful→用 stunning（绝美）、lush green（绿油油）、gorgeous
说放松只会 relax→用 hit pause（按下暂停键）、feel at peace（平和）、unwind（放松下来）
