避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The camera is easy to up hand.
✓ The camera is very easy to pick up and use.

「好上手」用 pick up and use，不是 up hand。

✕ I use the dial to adjust the length of focus.
✓ A dial lets you control the focal length.

焦段是 focal length；「调整焦段」是 control/change the focal length。

✕ It doesn't support Leica green mirror.
✓ It doesn't support Leica film profiles.

徕卡「滤镜」是 film profiles / picture profiles，不是 green mirror。

✕ The camera is not a bottom camera.
✓ It's not a camera you can rely on as your only body.

「保底相机」指不能作为唯一主力机，用 rely on as your only body 表达。
