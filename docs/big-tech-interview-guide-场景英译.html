避坑 PITFALLS

✕ ✕ 直译腔：I want to show my highlight.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：I want to highlight my key achievements.

highlight 作动词时搭配 achievements/results，不单独说 my highlight。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The interviewer wants to press you.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：The interviewer will push you hard.

push 比 press 更贴近面试语境。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：The offer is a purchase intent.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：An offer is an intent to hire.

intent to hire 是 HR 领域的标准说法。

✕ ✕ 直译腔：You have the active power.
✓ ✓ 地道说法：You hold the leverage.

表达「主动权」用 hold the leverage。
